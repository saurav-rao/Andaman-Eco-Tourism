About Manipur Parvat

Manipur Parvat, also known as Mount Harriet, is the third-highest peak in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands. It is renowned for its lush greenery, diverse flora and fauna, and panoramic views of the surrounding islands and sea.

Visiting Hours

The park is open to visitors from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM daily. It is advisable to visit during daylight hours to fully experience the natural beauty and for safety reasons.

How to Reach

Manipur Parvat is located approximately 20 kilometers from Port Blair. Visitors can reach the base by road, followed by a trek to the peak. Alternatively, ferries are available from Chatham Wharf to Bambooflat, followed by a drive to the park entrance.
