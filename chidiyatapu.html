About Chidiya Tapu

Chidiya Tapu, also known as "Bird Island," is a peaceful coastal village located about 25 kilometers from Port Blair in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands. It is renowned for its rich biodiversity, lush mangroves, and vibrant birdlife, making it a haven for nature enthusiasts and bird watchers. The sunsets here are breathtakingly beautiful, drawing visitors from around the world.

Visiting Hours

The area is accessible from early morning until evening. The Chidiya Tapu Biological Park operates from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

How to Reach

Chidiya Tapu is about 25 kilometers from Port Blair. Visitors can hire a taxi, take a bus, or rent a two-wheeler to reach the destination. The journey takes around 45 minutes to an hour and offers scenic views.

Amenities

The beach offers basic amenities like restrooms, changing rooms, and seating areas. Local vendors provide snacks and beverages. Additionally, designated swimming zones are safeguarded with nets for safety.

Best Time to Visit

The ideal time to visit Chidiya Tapu is between October and April when the weather is most favorable for outdoor activities and sightseeing.

Things to Do

Bird Watching

Observe indigenous and migratory birds in their natural habitat.

Trekking

Embark on a scenic trek to Munda Pahad for breathtaking views.

Water Activities

Enjoy snorkeling, scuba diving, and swimming in crystal-clear waters.

Nearby Places
