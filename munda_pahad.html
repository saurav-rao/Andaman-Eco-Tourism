About Munda Pahad Beach

Munda Pahad Beach is a serene destination located in Chidiya Tapu, Andaman. Known for its breathtaking views, this beach offers a tranquil escape from the bustling city life. The lush green surroundings and crystal-clear waters make it a perfect spot for nature lovers.

Visiting Hours

The beach is accessible from sunrise to sunset. Visitors are encouraged to leave the beach before nightfall as it is located in a remote area.

Amenities

The beach provides basic amenities such as restrooms, changing rooms, and seating areas. Food and beverages are available from local vendors near the beach.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Munda Pahad Beach is between October and April when the weather is pleasant, and the sea is calm, making it ideal for outdoor activities.

Things to Do

Trekking

Embark on a scenic trek through the lush green trails leading to the beach.

Photography

Capture stunning views of the coastline and surrounding greenery.

Relaxation

Enjoy the serene environment and unwind amidst nature.

Nearby Places

Chidiya Tapu

A nearby attraction known for its birdwatching opportunities and picturesque sunsets.

Biological Park

Explore the biodiversity of the Andaman Islands at this well-maintained park.
