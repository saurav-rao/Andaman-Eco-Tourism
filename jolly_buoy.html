About Jolly Buoy Island

Jolly Buoy Island is renowned for its vibrant coral reefs, clear waters, and unspoiled natural beauty. It is part of the Mahatma Gandhi Marine National Park and is a haven for snorkeling and diving enthusiasts. The island is free of plastic and is a perfect destination for eco-tourists.

Visiting Hours

Jolly Buoy Island is open to visitors from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. It is closed during the monsoon season, typically from May to October.

How to Reach

Jolly Buoy Island is accessible by boat from Wandoor Beach, located approximately 30 kilometers from Port Blair. Visitors must obtain permits in advance, as the island is part of a protected marine area.

Amenities

Visitors can enjoy basic amenities like changing rooms and restrooms. However, since the island is a protected zone, there are no shops or restaurants. Visitors are advised to carry their own food and water.

Best Time to Visit

The ideal time to visit Jolly Buoy Island is between November and April, when the weather is pleasant and the water visibility is excellent for marine activities.
